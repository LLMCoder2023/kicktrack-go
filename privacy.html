Privacy Policy

KickTrack Go

Effective date: 21 September 2026 · Last updated: 21 September 2026

Summary

KickTrack Go does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app has no network capability at all. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Everything you put into the app stays on your device.

Information we collect

None.

We do not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or diagnostics. There is no account to create, nothing to log into, and no way to send us your data through the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in its own private storage area on your device, so that it works the way you left it. This information is never transmitted anywhere.

WhatWhy
Copies of audio files you importSo a track keeps working after the original is moved, renamed, or removed from iCloud
Your track list, with each track's title, timing and tone settingsSo your setup is there the next time you open the app
Events and playlists you createSo a competition day you have planned is still planned tomorrow
Sound presets you saveSo you can reuse a tone you liked
App preferences, tutorial progress, and which optional features you have switched onSo the app does not re-ask or reset itself

All of this lives inside the app's private container. It is not visible to other apps, and it is not shared with us or anyone else.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

Audio files you choose

When you add music, iOS shows you its own file picker. The app receives only the specific file you select, and copies it into its private storage so playback keeps working later.

The app cannot browse your music library, your files, or your photos. It sees only what you hand it, when you hand it over.

Playback information shown by iOS

While a track is playing, the app tells iOS the track's title and length so that iOS can display it on your Lock Screen and operate playback from your AirPods, car, or Apple Watch.

This is a standard iOS feature. The information stays on your device and is handled by iOS, not by us. It is cleared when playback stops.

Apple's required-reason API disclosures

Apple requires apps to declare why they use certain system APIs. KickTrack Go declares two, both in its bundled privacy manifest:

  • User defaults - to store your preferences, tutorial progress, and which optional features are switched on.
  • System boot time - to keep the audio clock and the on-screen sound meter accurate while a track plays.

Neither is used to identify you or your device, and neither value is stored or transmitted.

Third parties

There are none.

The app links no third-party libraries, embeds no software development kits, and communicates with no servers - including ours. We do not have servers for this app.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children. It contains no advertising, no third-party content, no links out to the web, and no way to communicate with anyone through it.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete the personal information a company holds about you.

We hold none. There is nothing for us to show you, change, or delete, because nothing ever reaches us. The data on your device is entirely under your control and is removed completely when you delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect material changes; if that ever changed, it would be disclosed here and in the app's App Store listing before it took effect.
